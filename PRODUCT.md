# Product Context

## Product Purpose
Landing page for Leidy Carolina Granados Celis, a brand strategist, communications expert, and leadership coach. The page serves as her professional presence to attract entrepreneurial women seeking strategic guidance for their brands and projects.

## Register
brand

## Users
- **Primary**: Entrepreneurial women in Colombia (and Latin America) who are building purpose-driven brands and projects. They need strategic direction, clear communication, and sustainable growth. Age range 25-45, digitally active, values-driven.
- **Secondary**: Community leaders and social project founders looking for workshops, mentoring, and strategic communication support.

## Brand Voice & Tone
- Warm, empowering, professional but approachable
- Strategic and grounded, not corporate or cold
- Speaks to women as equals, not from a pedestal
- Inspirational without being generic or performative
- Spanish-language (Colombian)

## Anti-references
- Generic SaaS landing pages with hero metrics
- Cold corporate consulting sites
- Overly playful/informal coaching sites with excessive emojis
- Template-heavy Canva-style entrepreneur pages

## Strategic Principles
- Lead with clarity and purpose, not hype
- Show expertise through substance (real experience, real ecosystem)
- The brand ecosystem matters: El Vlog de Carito (main brand), Emprendiendo Juntas (community), Susurros (emotional wellness)
- Photography is real and personal, not stock
- Every section should feel intentional, not templated
